     8→     8→     8→
     9→     9→     9→## Core Principle
    10→    10→    10→
    11→    11→    11→The platform uses the following principle:
    12→    12→    12→
    13→    13→    13→**AI-on-Ingest, CPU-on-Match**
    14→    14→    14→
    15→    15→    15→This means:
    16→    16→    16→
    17→    17→    17→- AI or parser logic is used when a CV or job is uploaded.
    18→    18→    18→- The uploaded CV or job post is converted into structured data.
    19→    19→    19→- Structured data is saved in MySQL.
    20→    20→    20→- Matching is done using SQL joins and scoring logic.
    21→    21→    21→- AI is not used repeatedly during matching.
    22→    22→    22→- CV files are not permanently stored.
    23→    23→    23→
    24→    24→    24→This keeps the platform scalable, cheap, and fast.
    25→    25→    25→

    28→    28→    28→## 1. Product Vision
    29→    29→    29→
    30→    30→    30→The platform helps candidates discover jobs that match their real skills, qualifications, experience, and career interests.
    31→    31→    31→
    32→    32→    32→The goal is not to match only job titles.
    33→    33→    33→
    34→    34→    34→The goal is to understand:
    35→    35→    35→
    36→    36→    36→- What the candidate can do
    37→    37→    37→- What the job requires
    38→    38→    38→- What category of work the role belongs to
    39→    39→    39→- What subcategory or specialization the role belongs to
    40→    40→    40→- What skills are required
    41→    41→    41→- What qualifications are required
    42→    42→    42→- What the candidate is interested in
    43→    43→    43→- What jobs are realistic for the candidate
    44→    44→    44→
    45→    45→    45→---
    46→    46→    46→
    47→    47→    47→## 2. Matching Philosophy
    48→    48→    48→
    49→    49→    49→The system separates:
    50→    50→    50→
    51→    51→    51→- Organization type
    52→    52→    52→- Organization industry
    53→    53→    53→- Job category
    54→    54→    54→- Job subcategory
    55→    55→    55→- Role title
    56→    56→    56→- Skills
    57→    57→    57→- Tools
    58→    58→    58→- Qualifications
    59→    59→    59→- Experience
    60→    60→    60→- Candidate preferences
    61→    61→    61→
    62→    62→    62→### Example
    63→    63→    63→
    64→    64→    64→Kenyatta National Hospital is in the healthcare industry.
    65→    65→    65→
    66→    66→    66→But if they hire an HR Officer:
    67→    67→    67→
    68→    68→    68→- Organization industry: Healthcare
    69→    69→    69→- Job category: Human Resources
    70→    70→    70→- Job subcategory: HR Generalist or Recruitment
    71→    71→    71→- Role: HR Officer
    72→    72→    72→
    73→    73→    73→The candidate does not need to be a healthcare worker.
    74→    74→    74→
    75→    75→    75→They need to be an HR professional.
    76→    76→    76→
    77→    77→    77→Therefore:
    78→    78→    78→
    79→    79→    79→Industry is context.
    80→    80→    80→
    81→    81→    81→Category and subcategory are strong matching signals.
    82→    82→    82→
    83→    83→    83→Skills and qualifications are proof.
    84→    84→    84→
    85→    85→    85→---
    86→    86→    86→
    87→    87→    87→## 3. Why Industry Has Low Matching Weight
    88→    88→    88→
    89→    89→    89→A professional can work across many industries.
    90→    90→    90→
    91→    91→    91→Example:
    92→    92→    92→
    93→    93→    93→An HR Officer can work in:
    94→    94→    94→
    95→    95→    95→- Healthcare
    96→    96→    96→- Banking
    97→    97→    97→- Telecommunications
    98→    98→    98→- Education
    99→    99→    99→- Government
   100→   100→   100→- Manufacturing
   101→   101→   101→- NGO
   102→   102→   102→
   103→   103→   103→A Finance Officer can work in:
   104→   104→   104→
   105→   105→   105→- NGO
   106→   106→   106→- Government
   107→   107→   107→- Healthcare
   108→   108→   108→- Banking
   109→   109→   109→- Manufacturing
   110→   110→   110→- Education
   111→   111→   111→
   112→   112→   112→So industry should not dominate matching.
   113→   113→   113→
   114→   114→   114→Recommended use:
   115→   115→   115→
   116→   116→   116→- Industry familiarity is a small bonus.
   117→   117→   117→- It should not reject otherwise good candidates.
   118→   118→   118→- Category, subcategory, skills, and qualifications should carry more weight.
   119→   119→   119→
   120→   120→   120→---

   122→   122→   122→## 4. Final Matching Weights
   123→   123→   123→
   124→   124→   124→Recommended scoring model:
   125→   125→   125→
   126→   126→   126→```js
   127→   127→   127→const MATCH_WEIGHTS = {
   128→   128→   128→  categoryMatch: 25,
   129→   129→   129→  subcategoryMatch: 25,
   130→   130→   130→  skillsMatch: 25,
   131→   131→   131→  qualificationsMatch: 10,
   132→   132→   132→  experienceMatch: 10,
   133→   133→   133→  industryFamiliarity: 5
   134→   134→   134→};
   135→   135→   135→```
   136→   136→   136→
   137→   137→   137→Total: 100%
   138→   138→   138→
   139→   139→   139→Meaning:
   140→   140→   140→
   141→   141→   141→- Category match checks if the candidate is in the same career function.
   142→   142→   142→- Subcategory match checks specialization.
   143→   143→   143→- Skills match checks capability.
   144→   144→   144→- Qualifications match checks eligibility.
   145→   145→   145→- Experience match checks level.
   146→   146→   146→- Industry familiarity gives a small contextual bonus.

   151→   151→   151→
   152→   152→   152→### Candidate Upload Flow
   153→   153→   153→
   154→   154→   154→```txt
   155→   155→   155→Candidate uploads CV
   156→   156→   156→        ↓
   157→   157→   157→Backend receives file in memory
   158→   158→   158→        ↓
   159→   159→   159→Markitdown extracts Markdown/text
   160→   160→   160→        ↓
   161→   161→   161→Parser extracts structured data
   162→   162→   162→        ↓
   163→   163→   163→Optional AI fallback if parser confidence is low
   164→   164→   164→        ↓
   165→   165→   165→Normalize data against taxonomy
   166→   166→   166→        ↓
   167→   167→   167→Save structured profile to MySQL
   168→   168→   168→        ↓
   169→   169→   169→Discard uploaded file
   170→   170→   170→        ↓
   171→   171→   171→Run CPU-based matching
   172→   172→   172→        ↓
   173→   173→   173→Save top matches
   174→   174→   174→        ↓
   175→   175→   175→Return candidate profile + matches
   176→   176→   176→```
   177→   177→   177→

   186→   186→   186→        ↓
   187→   187→   187→Parser or AI extracts structured job profile
   188→   188→   188→        ↓
   189→   189→   189→Normalize against taxonomy
   190→   190→   190→        ↓

   200→   200→   200→## 6. No Media Storage Rule
   201→   201→   201→
   202→   202→   202→CV files should not be permanently stored.
   203→   203→   203→
   204→   204→   204→The file should be:
   205→   205→   205→
   206→   206→   206→1. Uploaded.
   207→   207→   207→2. Processed in memory.
   208→   208→   208→3. Converted to text.
   209→   209→   209→4. Discarded.
   210→   210→   210→
   211→   211→   211→The database stores:
   212→   212→   212→
   213→   213→   213→- Extracted name
   214→   214→   214→- Email
   215→   215→   215→- Phone
   216→   216→   216→- Skills
   217→   217→   217→- Education
   218→   218→   218→- Work experience
   219→   219→   219→- Qualifications
   220→   220→   220→- Career category
   221→   221→   221→- Subcategories
   222→   222→   222→- Matching data
   223→   223→   223→
   224→   224→   224→The database should not store the original CV file unless a future business requirement requires it.
   225→   225→   225→
   226→   226→   226→Optional:
   227→   227→   227→
   228→   228→   228→You may store raw extracted CV text for debugging for a short period, for example 30 days.

   373→   373→   373→## 9. Taxonomy Model
   374→   374→   374→
   375→   375→   375→The taxonomy is the foundation of the matching engine.
   376→   376→   376→
   377→   377→   377→It stores:
   378→   378→   378→
   379→   379→   379→- Organization types
   380→   380→   380→- Industries
   381→   381→   381→- Job categories
   382→   382→   382→- Subcategories
   383→   383→   383→- Roles
   384→   384→   384→- Skills
   385→   385→   385→- Tools
   386→   386→   386→- Qualifications
   387→   387→   387→- Certifications
   388→   388→   388→- Specializations
   389→   389→   389→
   390→   390→   390→### 9.1 taxonomy_items
   391→   391→   391→
   392→   392→   392→```sql
   393→   393→   393→CREATE TABLE taxonomy_items (
   394→   394→   394→  id BIGINT UNSIGNED PRIMARY KEY AUTO_INCREMENT,
   395→   395→   395→
   396→   396→   396→  type ENUM(
   397→   397→   397→    'ORGANIZATION_TYPE',
   398→   398→   398→    'INDUSTRY',
   399→   399→   399→    'CATEGORY',
   400→   400→   400→    'SUBCATEGORY',
   401→   401→   401→    'ROLE',
   402→   402→   402→    'SKILL',
   403→   403→   403→    'TOOL',
   404→   404→   404→    'QUALIFICATION',
   405→   405→   405→    'CERTIFICATION',
   406→   406→   406→    'SPECIALIZATION',
   407→   407→   407→    'REGULATION',
   408→   408→   408→    'COMPETENCY'
   409→   409→   409→  ) NOT NULL,
   410→   410→   410→
   411→   411→   411→  value VARCHAR(120) NOT NULL,
   412→   412→   412→  label VARCHAR(255) NOT NULL,
   413→   413→   413→
   414→   414→   414→  parent_id BIGINT UNSIGNED NULL,
   415→   415→   415→
   416→   416→   416→  description TEXT NULL,
   417→   417→   417→
   418→   418→   418→  is_active BOOLEAN NOT NULL DEFAULT TRUE,
   419→   419→   419→
   420→   420→   420→  created_at TIMESTAMP DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP,
   421→   421→   421→  updated_at TIMESTAMP DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP ON UPDATE CURRENT_TIMESTAMP,
   422→   422→   422→
   423→   423→   423→  UNIQUE KEY unique_taxonomy_value (type, value),
   424→   424→   424→
   425→   425→   425→  INDEX idx_taxonomy_type (type),
   426→   426→   426→  INDEX idx_taxonomy_parent (parent_id),
   427→   427→   427→
   428→   428→   428→  CONSTRAINT fk_taxonomy_parent
   429→   429→   429→    FOREIGN KEY (parent_id)
   430→   430→   430→    REFERENCES taxonomy_items(id)
   431→   431→   431→    ON DELETE SET NULL
   432→   432→   432→);
   433→   433→   433→```
   434→   434→   434→
   435→   435→   435→Example rows:
   436→   436→   436→
   437→   437→   437→```txt
   438→   438→   438→CATEGORY: HUMAN_RESOURCES
   439→   439→   439→SUBCATEGORY: RECRUITMENT
   440→   440→   440→SKILL: CANDIDATE_SCREENING
   441→   441→   441→TOOL: QUICKBOOKS
   442→   442→   442→QUALIFICATION: BACHELOR_DEGREE
   443→   443→   443→INDUSTRY: HEALTHCARE
   444→   444→   444→```

   482→   482→   482→### 9.3 taxonomy_relationships
   483→   483→   483→
   484→   484→   484→This supports adjacent career relationships.
   485→   485→   485→
   486→   486→   486→Example:
   487→   487→   487→
   488→   488→   488→```txt
   489→   489→   489→Finance is related to Banking
   490→   490→   490→Finance is related to Insurance
   491→   491→   491→HR is related to Administration
   492→   492→   492→Media is related to Marketing Communications
   493→   493→   493→```
   494→   494→   494→
   495→   495→   495→```sql
   496→   496→   496→CREATE TABLE taxonomy_relationships (
   497→   497→   497→  id BIGINT UNSIGNED PRIMARY KEY AUTO_INCREMENT,
   498→   498→   498→
   499→   499→   499→  source_item_id BIGINT UNSIGNED NOT NULL,
   500→   500→   500→  target_item_id BIGINT UNSIGNED NOT NULL,
   501→   501→   501→
   502→   502→   502→  relationship_type ENUM(
   503→   503→   503→    'RELATED',
   504→   504→   504→    'ADJACENT',
   505→   505→   505→    'TRANSFERABLE',
   506→   506→   506→    'PARENT_CHILD',
   507→   507→   507→    'SIMILAR'
   508→   508→   508→  ) NOT NULL DEFAULT 'RELATED',
   509→   509→   509→
   510→   510→   510→  weight DECIMAL(4,2) NOT NULL DEFAULT 1.00,
   511→   511→   511→
   512→   512→   512→  created_at TIMESTAMP DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP,
   513→   513→   513→
   514→   514→   514→  UNIQUE KEY unique_relationship (source_item_id, target_item_id, relationship_type),
   515→   515→   515→
   516→   516→   516→  CONSTRAINT fk_relationship_source
   517→   517→   517→    FOREIGN KEY (source_item_id)
   518→   518→   518→    REFERENCES taxonomy_items(id)
   519→   519→   519→    ON DELETE CASCADE,
   520→   520→   520→
   521→   521→   521→  CONSTRAINT fk_relationship_target
   522→   522→   522→    FOREIGN KEY (target_item_id)
